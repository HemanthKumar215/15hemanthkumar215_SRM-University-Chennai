Key Clinical Challenges in Type 2 Diabetes

  • Postprandial Glycemic Excursions: Rapid glucose surges exceeding 180 mg/dL induce acute endothelial oxidative stress and cardiovascular complications.
  • Delayed Intervention Horizon: Conventional fingerstick checks and lagging continuous glucose alerts notify patients only after hyper- or hypoglycemia has already occurred.
  • Lack of Actionable Counterfactuals: Clinicians lack quantitative tools to answer: "Will a 20-minute post-meal walk safely attenuate this glucose spike?"
  • Heterogeneity of South Asian Phenotype: Higher body-fat percentage at lower BMI with premature beta-cell dysfunction demands tailored digital twin modeling.

Deep Learning Pipeline

End-to-End Multimodal Neural Architecture

Technical Specification

Neural Fusion Network Specifications

  • Dynamic Sensor Lookback (GRU): Processes 288 consecutive 5-minute timesteps across 5 channels (CGM, HR, HRV, steps, sleep stage). Two stacked GRU layers (hidden_dim=64, dropout=0.3); the final hidden state + LayerNorm yields a 64-dim dynamic embedding.
  • Static EHR Branch (MLP): Encodes 27 static features (age, sex, BMI, HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipids, BP, diagnoses, 7 medication classes, family history, genetic risk score, smoking, activity). 2-layer MLP (27 to 64 to 32, LayerNorm, ReLU) yields a 32-dim static embedding.
  • Cross-Modal Fusion Head: Concatenation layer (96 dims) feeding a classification MLP with calibrated sigmoid activation. Predicts spike within 2h given baseline glucose below 180 mg/dL.
  • Modality Dropout: Random masking of HR/HRV/steps/sleep channels (p=0.3) during pretraining guarantees stability even when wearable signals (e.g. sleep/HR) are temporarily missing.

Zero Data Leakage Guarantees

Strict patient-level disjoint partitioning ensures 0% patient cross-contamination. Strict temporal causality dictates that the 24h lookback window strictly terminates before the 2h prediction horizon.

Clinical Calibration Sanity

HbA1c-to-mean-glucose Pearson correlation achieves r = 0.9798 (p < 0.001), perfectly aligning with Nathan et al. 2008 ADAG trial standards.

Clinical Nuance: The Fair Baseline Story

  • Instantaneous Proximity Bias: When a patient's current glucose is 175 mg/dL, predicting a spike (>180) within 2h is near-trivial for tabular decision trees (0.9833 AUROC).
  • Fair Baseline (No Current Glucose): Removing instantaneous proximity forces models to evaluate underlying trajectory and metabolic state (LightGBM reaches 0.9699).
  • What Multimodal Fusion Uniquely Adds: While LightGBM relies heavily on static hand-engineered scalars, Deep Fusion processes raw temporal sequences, resists sensor noise, and supports dynamic counterfactual What-If simulation.
  • No Metric Inflation: All values are read directly and immutably from reports/metrics.json.

Trust & Safety

Multimodal Feature Attribution (Occlusion)

Interpretability

Opening the Deep Learning Black Box

  • Plain-Language Clinical Rationale: Plain-English synthesis of why a specific patient was flagged as elevated risk.
  • Signed Feature Impacts (percentage points): Each wearable channel and EHR feature is replaced by the population average and the model is re-run; bars show the change in spike probability, protective (green) vs risk-raising (coral).
  • Multimodal Cross-Attributions: Separates acute drivers from the last 24 h of wearables (glucose trace, HR, HRV, steps, sleep) from chronic EHR drivers (HbA1c, BMI, medications).
  • Doctor-Centric Clarity: Enables the clinician to validate whether the model's reasoning matches metabolic pathophysiology.

Ethics & Governance

Data Provenance, Limitations & Ethical Guardrails

Governance

Transparent Data Provenance

  • Pretraining (Synthetic): 15,000 synthetic patient-days (500 patients × 30 days) calibrated to clinical literature (Nathan et al. 2008 ADAG equations, gamma meal curves, circadian sleep/HR).
  • Adapter Validation: Evaluated on simulated real-format files representing AI-READI, CGMacros, and Shanghai schemas. Honestly relabeled as "simulated real-format" to avoid false claims of physical patient trials.
  • Zero Hallucinated Numbers: Every dashboard metric and README table is synchronized strictly from reports/metrics.json.

Ethical Limitations & Clinical Disclaimers

  • Investigational Prototype: GlycoTwin is a research prototype decision-support tool, not an FDA/CDSCO cleared autonomous diagnostic system.
  • Clinician-in-the-Loop: All recommendations (walks, carb modifications) require clinical oversight and cannot substitute for emergency diabetes protocols.
  • Subgroup Fairness: Subgroups with fewer than 30 cases are explicitly greyed out with warning notices to avoid misleading assertions.
